FAQ

How does payment work?

A deposit is required at kickoff to reserve your slot. The balance is paid on delivery, once you've approved the project.

Do I own my store or website?

Yes, 100%. You are the sole owner of your Shopify store or of your custom website's code, your admin access and your domain name.

What if I need help after delivery?

I hand over all logins so you can run your store or website yourself, and I stay reachable for any technical question, especially during your drops.
