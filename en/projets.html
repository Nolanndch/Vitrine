InfluLab IA

Web app & AI

Personal product

4 / 4
My own SaaS product: a web app that analyses content creators' stats on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Twitch. Users drop their stats export and the AI turns it into a concrete report: what works, what holds them back and what to post next.

What I delivered

In-browser analysis of CSV exports

columns from each platform are recognised automatically, and only a numerical summary (averages, medians and top titles) is sent to the AI.

Detailed AI report

standout video, strengths, weak point, five-day action plan and PDF export.

Video script generator

(hook, body, call to action) from a simple idea.

Free, Pro and Creator plans

, monthly or yearly, with an analysis history.

Bilingual FR / EN website

, with a blog of guides and free tools optimised for search engines (SEO).
