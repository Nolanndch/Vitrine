01 / CLIENT PROJECTShopify & streetwear drops

7venworld

The project

7venworld is an independent Parisian streetwear brand. The store is designed for limited collection launches (capsules, graphic tees, flare jeans, summer packs): clearly announced drops, an easy-to-browse catalogue and fast checkout.

What I delivered

  • Dynamic scrolling banner to announce drop dates and offers.
  • Filterable catalogue by category (t-shirts, summer packs, caps, denim).
  • Express checkout enabled (Shop Pay & Apple Pay) so buyers can pay in seconds during drops.
  • Custom Liquid code to add sections that standard themes didn't offer.

02 / CLIENT PROJECTShopify & fashion

Ambleme

The project

Ambleme is a clothing brand with a strong identity, now on its third drop. The goal was a clean store that showcases the visual world of its collections while addressing customers' concerns before they buy.

  • Interactive FAQ built into the product page, answering questions before they're even asked: delivery times, parcel tracking, restocks and care tips.

04 / PERSONAL PRODUCTWeb app & AI

InfluLab IA

The project

My own SaaS product: a web app that analyses content creators' stats on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Twitch. Users drop their stats export and the AI turns it into a concrete report: what works, what holds them back and what to post next.

What I delivered

  • In-browser analysis of CSV exports : each platform's columns are recognised automatically, and only a numeric summary (averages, medians, top titles) is sent to the AI.
  • Detailed AI report : standout video, strengths, weak spot, 5-day action plan and PDF export.
  • Video script generator (hook, body, call to action) from a simple idea.
  • Free, Pro and Creator plans, monthly or yearly, with an analysis history.
  • Bilingual FR / EN website, with a blog of guides and free tools optimised for search engines (SEO).
