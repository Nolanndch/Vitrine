What I've built.

Four live projects, built for client brands and for my own product. Each one answers a specific need: collection releases in drops, a polished launch page for a high-end product, or a SaaS app connected to AI.

01 / CLIENT PROJECT Shopify & streetwear drops

7venworld

7venworld is an independent Parisian streetwear brand. The store is designed for limited collection launches (capsules, graphic tees, flare jeans, summer packs): clearly announced drops, an easy-to-browse catalogue and fast checkout.

What I delivered
  • Dynamic scrolling banner to announce drop dates and offers.
  • Filterable catalogue by category (t-shirts, summer packs, caps, denim).
  • Express checkout enabled (Shop Pay & Apple Pay) so buyers can pay in seconds during drops.
  • Custom Liquid code to add sections that standard themes didn't offer.
Role:
Design, build, setup

02 / CLIENT PROJECT Shopify & fashion

Ambleme

Ambleme is a clothing brand with a strong identity, now on its third drop. The goal was a clean store that showcases the visual world of its collections while addressing customers' concerns before they buy.

What I delivered
  • Collection lookbook to showcase the pieces (long-sleeve tees, accessories, temporary tattoos).
  • Interactive FAQ built into the product page, answering questions before they're even asked: delivery times, parcel tracking, restocks and care tips.
  • Polished mobile experience with intuitive navigation and smooth transitions.

Design, build, setup

03 / CUSTOM BUILD Landing page & watches

Calciori

Custom launch page for the Sopra, the very first model from a contemporary watch brand. Everything fits on a single fast-loading page, built to grab attention and build anticipation before the drop.

What I delivered
  • Detailed spec sheet — brushed 316L stainless steel, 41 mm case, sapphire crystal, engraved caseback.
  • Lightweight, fast page — hand-coded in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no heavy dependencies.
  • Drop countdown and an early-access sign-up form.
Role & tech stack:
Design & code · HTML/CSS/JS · Vercel

04 / PERSONAL PRODUCT Web app & AI

InfluLab IA

My own product: a full web app for content creators on YouTube, TikTok, Twitch and Instagram. Users import their metrics and the AI turns them into concrete recommendations to grow their audience and revenue.

What I delivered
  • Large language model (LLM) API integration to generate tailored action plans.
  • Interactive dashboards with audience analytics charts.
  • Subscriptions managed and secured with Stripe Billing.
  • Blog and resource centre optimised for organic search (SEO).
Tech stack:
React, Next.js, AI API, Stripe, Vercel
