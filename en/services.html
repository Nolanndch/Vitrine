01 / Improve my store

Shopify fixes & redesign

3 days to 1 week

Your store already exists but looks unprofessional or no longer matches your new collections? I work directly on your theme to refresh it, clean up the code, improve mobile usability and add what's missing.

Typical work:

  • Scrolling drop-announcement banner
  • New lookbook or FAQ sections
  • Product page redesign to sell more
  • Faster loading speed

Included: visual fixes, mobile adjustments and a clean-up of your existing theme, plus hero, announcement bar, size guide, catalogue filters, lookbook and FAQ. Drop countdown, bilingual store and speed & SEO optimisation are available as options.

What you can prepare

  • Your store URL and the required access
  • What needs improving and a few visual references
  • Your content and preferred launch date

The redesign works with your existing Shopify store. If you are starting from scratch or changing platforms, the scope will be reviewed before quoting.

02 / Launch my store

Complete Shopify store

1 to 2 weeks

For independent brands, streetwear labels and creators launching their business or preparing a major drop. I design and set up your store from A to Z. You leave with a working store, ready to take payments and handle shipping.

What's included:

  • Mockup and theme customisation
  • Collection structure & product pages
  • Shopify payments enabled: cards, Apple Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal
  • Shipping setup (Colissimo, Mondial Relay…)
  • Domain setup & launch
  • Every section and option included: hero, announcement bar, size guide, filters, lookbook, FAQ, drop countdown, bilingual store, speed & SEO

The Shopify subscription, the domain purchase and any paid apps remain at your expense.

What you can prepare

  • Your visual identity, photos and copy
  • Your catalogue: products, prices, variants and stock
  • Your shipping methods and business details

You can then manage your products, orders and stock from Shopify. The platform subscription is separate from my service.

Before getting started

Which service should I choose if I am unsure?

Start with the questionnaire or simply describe your goal. We will clarify the useful features, solution and scope together before any commitment.

Do I need to have my copy and photos ready?

You can contact me before everything is ready. To move forward with the mockup and build, we will identify the content you need to provide: visual identity, copy, photos and product details.

What if the project changes?

The quote sets the scope and schedule before work begins. Any additional requirement is discussed before implementation to confirm its impact on the budget and timeline.

What if I need help after delivery?

You receive access to your store or your website’s code. I remain available for technical questions and launch support; any additional work is scoped together.
